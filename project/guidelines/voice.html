How we sound

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. "We design and operate…". State what we do; don't oversell. British spelling: organisation, modernise, optimise.

Case

Sentence case for every headline, button, label and chip. Uppercase only through CSS, on eyebrows, labels and action lines. The four practice areas keep their title case names.

Punctuation

No em dashes, and no spaced en dashes doing their job: use a comma, a colon, a full stop or a new sentence. No exclamation marks. No emoji. "·" separates items in a meta line. Deltas use the minus sign: −18ms.

Numbering

Two digits and a space: "01 What we do", "02 Platforms". Steps count 01 to 05. Legal sections keep 1. and 3.1.

Calls to action

A verb and what follows, then a typed →: "Start a project →", "Send enquiry →", "Draft the blueprint →". Busy labels are present tense: "Sending", "Drafting", "Connecting". A navigation keeps its own label while it loads.

Placeholders

Ask the question the field answers: "What are you building or changing, where does it stand today, and is there a date that matters?" Never "Enter your message".

Errors

Say what to do: "Enter a valid email.", "Tell us a little about the project." Never a code. Never a sentence that blames.

Never say

cutting-edge · innovative · transformative · synergy · empower · unlock · leverage · solutions (as a noun on its own) · next-generation · world-class
