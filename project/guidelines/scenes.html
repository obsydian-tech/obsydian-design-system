Light

The pointer is the lamp: a bone point light follows it (the orb's key runs 4 to 26 with presence). Violet only rims from behind ("violet catches the back edges, never the tops"). A weak bone fill from the upper left. RoomEnvironment through PMREM, intensity 0.2 to 0.32. ACES filmic, exposure 1.05.

Material

Polished obsidian: MeshPhysicalMaterial, clearcoat 1, clearcoat roughness 0.05 to 0.1, roughness 0.32 to 0.36 and metalness 0.15 to 0.2 on the slabs and blocks; the orb keeps its model's own roughness and normal maps, with its veins lit by --scene-ember. Bodies are --scene-obsidian; a body that steps back lerps to --scene-obsidian-dim. Glow is an additive violet radial pool under the lit thing.

Interaction

Hover lifts and lights; a tap or click selects; a mouse drag turns the scene; touch only taps, so the page still scrolls. Names are real buttons laid over the canvas in sentence case, with a violet-hi underline when selected and a 1px violet-hi focus outline.

Cost

three.js loads lazily after first paint. Models are meshopt-compressed (the orb is 368 KB). Pixel ratio at most 2. Rendering pauses off screen. The canvas fades up over 1.4s and settles 12px over 1.8s on --ease-page.

Fallback

No WebGL, or any error, swaps in the flat SVG: the orb shows assets/shard.svg, the stack its isometric diagram, the systems their topology map. The page never breaks for want of a GPU.

Reduced motion

No idle spin, sway, parallax or travelling packets. Selection, hover and drag still work, because they answer the person rather than move on their own.
