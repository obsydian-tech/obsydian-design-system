01 Rules

Ten rules
everything obeys.

They come from how the site already works and from decisions taken while building it. A new page, component or agent brief follows them before anything else here.

  1. 01

    Black is the canvas

    Near-black surfaces do the work, bone is the text and violet is the one accent. There is never a new colour; the only exception is a muted red on a field that needs fixing.

  2. 02

    Hairlines, not boxes

    Sections, lists and grids are drawn with 1px bone lines. Nothing wraps content in a card, and nothing casts a shadow for depth; only what floats (the nav pill, menus, toasts) casts one.

  3. 03

    One violet action per viewport

    The glossy primary is the next thing we want someone to do. Everything else is a ghost pill or an underlined link. Two filled violet buttons never sit together.

  4. 04

    Every button shows it is working

    From the press until what it started lands, the control carries a spinner, is busy and ignores a second press. A submit says what it is doing ("Sending"); a navigation keeps its own label.

  5. 05

    Fields look like fields

    Recessed wells, sentence-case labels above them, placeholders that ask the question. No card around a form, no tiny uppercase labels, no empty boxes.

  6. 06

    One typeface

    General Sans for everything. Headlines at 500, tight and balanced. Uppercase only for eyebrows, labels and action lines. No monospace on the site.

  7. 07

    The mark is the mark

    Only the official shard, from one file and one component. Never redrawn, never re-faceted, never a gradient square.

  8. 08

    The pointer is the lamp

    Light on the hero and in every scene follows the pointer, and drifts on its own when there is none. Violet only rims from behind; it never lights a top face.

  9. 09

    Motion is slow, quiet and optional

    Sections reveal once over 600ms, hovers take 250ms, routes morph in 450ms. Every loop and rise has a reduced-motion state.

  10. 10

    Say it plainly

    Short sentences, concrete nouns, sentence case. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no banned words. State what we do; don't oversell.

04 Type

General Sans,
for everything.

{{count.type}} roles from project/tokens/typography.css. Headlines are 500 with negative tracking and balance across lines; reading text is 400 at -0.01em. General Sans stands in for Söhne until the licence exists.

05 Shape

Four radii,
and the hairline.

Corners are a tight 6, a 10 for fields, a 16 for the larger wells, or a full pill; the primary button alone is 18 (22 large). Structure is a 1px hairline. Shadows belong only to what floats.

Depth

Four recipes. Anything else that seems to need a shadow needs a hairline instead.

06 Motion

Slow, quiet,
and never required.

One ease-out curve carries nearly everything. Sections rise 20px once as they enter; lists stagger 18px; routes morph with the view transition API; film grain drifts at 3.8% over the page.

Under reduced motion nothing rises, drifts or loops: the orb stops turning, the stagger and the grain stop, the splash shows the finished mark. Colour and opacity still fade, and a busy spinner slows to 1.6s rather than stopping, because a still ring reads as broken.

07 Space and layout

Room to
breathe.

One 1440px column with a 20 to 40px gutter. Two breakpoints: below 980px two-column layouts stack; below 820px everything is one column, the nav becomes a card and sections drop to 72px.

08 Voice

State what we do.
Don't oversell.

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. First person plural. The build refuses to publish this system if any card, screen or page uses a banned word or an em dash.

Case

Sentence case for every headline, button, label and chip. Uppercase only through CSS, on eyebrows, labels and action lines. The four practice areas keep their title-case names.

Punctuation

No em dashes, and no spaced en dashes standing in for one: use a comma, a colon, a full stop or a new sentence. No exclamation marks, no emoji. A middle dot separates items in a meta line; deltas use the minus sign, −18ms.

Numbering

Two digits and a space: 01 What we do, 02 Platforms. Steps count 01 to 05. Legal sections keep 1. and 3.1.

Calls to action

A verb and what follows, then a typed arrow: Start a project →, Send enquiry →. Busy labels are present tense: Sending, Drafting, Connecting.

Placeholders

Ask the question the field answers: What are you building or changing, where does it stand today, and is there a date that matters? Never Enter your message.

Errors

Say what to do: Enter a valid email. Never a code, never blame. An error about the whole form is a calm sentence on a violet bar, not a red box.

12 Decisions

What changed
since the first version.

The June system described a site that has since moved on. These are the decisions this version records, newest first, with the website change that made each one.

  1. 6 Oct 2026
    Every click shows it is working

    A 2px route bar for navigations slower than 150ms, and a spinner on the pressed control until it lands. Site PRs #10 and #11.

  2. 4 Oct 2026
    The splash is the official shard

    The splash had drawn its own cube-like hexagon. It now assembles the real mark through one shared component: the outline traces, the face fills, the edge ignites behind a spark, one sheen passes. Site PR #9.

  3. 1 Oct 2026
    Fields are recessed wells

    The underline fields read as text, not as somewhere to type. Fields became wells one step lighter than the page, with a full hairline, an inner shadow and a violet focus ring. Site PR #7.

  4. 1 Oct 2026
    Stack and Systems are three.js scenes

    Obsidian slabs and glass layers lit by the pointer, with names as real buttons over the canvas and the old SVG kept as the fallback. Site PRs #6 and #7.

  5. 29 Sep 2026
    Forms are editorial

    The contact form and the blueprint studio read as generated templates. No card, sentence-case labels, larger type, guiding prompts, choice chips, a numbered "what happens next", a spinner on submit. Site PRs #4 and #5.

  6. 29 Sep 2026
    The hero is an obsidian orb

    A real 3D crystal in place of the flat SVG shard: it follows the pointer light, glows under hover, spins on a drag. It loads lazily and falls back to the flat stone. Site PR #3.

  7. Jul 2026
    One typeface

    JetBrains Mono was retired. Eyebrows and figures are General Sans, uppercase and tracked, with tabular numbers.

13 Drift

Where the site
disagrees with itself.

Where the live site had several versions of one thing, this system picked one. These are the places the site still differs, for the website to fix; the system is the reference. The full list with file paths is DRIFT.md in this repo.

Colour

  • Purple glows. The hero and the closing call glow in a stock purple (168, 85, 247), not brand violet.
  • Untokened alphas. About thirty one-off bone and violet strengths; the system has four bone steps and five violet alphas.
  • Two error reds and a green. The sign-in page uses a stock red and green; the contact form's muted red is the one error colour.
  • Violet in copy. Privacy links and transcript lines are violet at rest; links are bone with a hairline.

Type and copy

  • Headline weight. The sign-in page, the /intro page and the signed-in pages set headlines at 600; the system's headlines are 500.
  • Tiny uppercase labels. 9 and 10px uppercase labels on the sign-in form, the agent page and the signed-in pages; form labels are 15px sentence case.
  • Em dashes. Still in the Operate copy, the sign-in page, the signed-in pages and two email subjects.
  • Label tracking. 0.1 to 0.18em in places; labels are 0.12em, action lines 0.08em.

Shape and components

  • Four spinners. White, bone, current-colour and a still one; there is one spinner, in current colour.
  • Hero hairlines. Some page heroes rule their bottom, most their top; heroes rule their top.
  • Off-scale radii. The app badge (14px), focus outlines (4px) and the visualiser (26 to 28px).
  • Shard copies. The nav, footer and signed-in pages paste their own shard; one outline is at 26% instead of 22%.

Behaviour

  • Active nav link. The desktop nav never shows the current page.
  • Focus. Capability rows and signal panels have no visible keyboard focus; the mobile menu does not hold focus.
  • Field errors. Not tied to their field with aria-describedby or aria-invalid.
  • Reduced motion. Section reveals, the drafting steps and the visualiser's frame still move.

For engineers

Using it
in code.

project/ is the source. The build checks its own rules, compiles the components, and renders this page from the token files.

Tokens

project/styles.css imports every token file. The website keeps the same names as SCSS variables; obsydian-tokens.scss is generated from the CSS on every build.

Components

React in project/components, each with a .d.ts and a .prompt.md. The site's Angular components mirror them; a new pattern is added here first, then ported.

Agents

Coding and design agents start at project/SKILL.md, then project/readme.md. Run npm run preview to build and serve on port 4400; npm test runs the rules.
